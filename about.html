About Novara

Who we are

Novara Global Limited is a strategic advisory firm operating at the intersection of international trade, regulatory compliance, public policy, governance and stakeholder engagement.

We work with businesses, governments, development organizations and industry stakeholders to translate complex regulatory and policy requirements into practical strategies that improve market access, institutional performance and sustainable growth.

Novara Global is an institution led by a multidisciplinary policy and trade professional — built to operate as a lasting advisory practice, not a single consultant's personal brand.

Our approach

Every engagement moves through the same five stages, adapted to the client's context.

Understand

We study the regulatory, institutional and market context a client is operating in.

Assess

We identify gaps in compliance, readiness and market positioning.

Strategize

We design a practical path suited to the client's resources and goals.

Implement

We work alongside clients to put systems, documentation and capacity in place.

Measure

We track progress and adjust the strategy as markets and regulations evolve.
